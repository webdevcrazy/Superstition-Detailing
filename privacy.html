Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Superstition Detailing ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information when you visit superstitiondetailing.com (the "Site") or use our mobile detailing services. By using the Site or our services, you agree to this Privacy Policy and our Terms of Service. The Site and our services are intended for individuals 18 years of age or older.

1. Information We Collect

Information you provide. When you submit our quote form, call, text, email, or message us on social media, we collect the information you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address, city, vehicle type, requested service, and any message you include.

Information collected automatically. We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the Site. Google Analytics collects information such as your approximate location, device and browser type, pages viewed, time on site, and referral source, using cookies and similar technologies. This data is aggregated and does not directly identify you to us.

Service records. If you become a customer, we may keep records of services performed, appointment dates, locations served, payments, and before/after photographs of your vehicle for quality and dispute-resolution purposes.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to: respond to quote requests and inquiries; schedule, perform, and follow up on services; process payments; improve the Site and our services; maintain business records; protect against fraud and abuse; and comply with legal obligations. We may contact you by phone, text, or email about your inquiry or appointment. We do not send marketing messages without your consent, and you may opt out of any non-essential communication at any time.

3. How Information Is Shared

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing. We share information only with service providers who help us operate, and only as needed:

FormSubmit — processes our quote form and delivers your submission to our email inbox. Google — provides analytics (Google Analytics 4), fonts, and our business listing/reviews. GitHub Pages — hosts this Site and may log standard technical data (such as IP addresses) as part of normal web hosting. We may also disclose information if required by law, to enforce our Terms of Service, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of our business or others.

4. Cookies and Analytics Choices

The Site uses cookies primarily through Google Analytics. You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings, use your browser's "Do Not Track" or Global Privacy Control features, or install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout. The Site remains fully functional without analytics cookies.

5. Data Retention

We retain quote inquiries and correspondence for as long as needed to respond and for a reasonable period thereafter for business records. Customer service records are retained as needed for warranty, accounting, tax, and legal purposes. We delete or anonymize information when it is no longer needed.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to opt out of certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, email us at contact@superstitiondetailing.com with your request. We will verify your identity and respond within the time required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

7. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect your information, including serving the Site over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

The Site and our services are not directed to anyone under 18 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a minor has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the current version. Material changes will be posted on this page, and your continued use of the Site after changes are posted constitutes acceptance.
