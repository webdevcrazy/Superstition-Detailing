Common questions

Does Superstition Detailing come to my location?

Yes. We're a fully mobile car detailing service — we bring professional-grade equipment and products to your home or office anywhere in Mesa, AZ and nearby East Valley areas, including Gilbert, Chandler, Tempe, Apache Junction, and Queen Creek.

What is included in a trunk-to-front detail?

Every inch of the vehicle: cargo areas, floorboards, air vents, upholstery, interior surfaces, and exterior paint — for cars, trucks, and SUVs. Nothing gets skipped.

Is the detailing process safe for my driveway?

Yes. We offer eco-friendly, water-efficient methods designed to deliver superior results while staying safe for your driveway and easy on the Arizona desert.

How much does mobile detailing cost?

Packages start at $89 for The Maintenance Wash, $150 for The Interior Detail, $250 for The Essential Refresh, and $450 for The Showroom Restoration & Sanitize. Starting prices are for standard sedans — trucks, SUVs, and heavily soiled vehicles are quoted a little higher, and your final price is confirmed before any work begins.

Do you use my water?

We hook up to a spigot at your location, but whatever water we use is on us — you'll see a credit right on your invoice covering the cost. Between that and our water-efficient methods, your water bill won't even notice we stopped by.

How do I book an appointment?

Call or text (480) 569-4666, or send a DM on Instagram. We schedule appointments that fit around your day.

Are you connected to other "Superstition" businesses?

No — Superstition Detailing is an independent, locally owned mobile detailing service named for the Superstition Mountains east of Mesa. We're not affiliated with any collision center, car wash, or other business with "Superstition" in the name.
